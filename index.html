The problem

Qualification eats the week before writing even starts

Bid writers and small consultancies screen 15–30 tender alerts a week. Most of that manual reading confirms what you suspected — wrong sector, wrong value, wrong geography, already awarded. BidScreen does the initial qualification read for you, against a business profile you configure once.

What BidScreen is not: it's a tender qualification tool, not a bid writing tool. It helps you make the bid/no-bid decision — it does not write your response, and the screening is an AI-generated aid to your judgement, not a substitute for it. If you bid once or twice a year, the free tier's API key setup probably isn't worth the friction until Pro launches.

How it works

Three steps, no uploads

1

Open a tender notice

Any portal — Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, SAM.gov, CanadaBuys, AusTender, ProContract, or anywhere else a tender detail page loads. Attachment-heavy portal? Paste the text instead.

2

Click the BidScreen icon

BidScreen reads only the page you're viewing — no browsing history access, no background scanning — and checks it against your certifications, insurance, turnover, and geographic reach.

3

Get the screening

Fit level with compatibility score, a colour-coded hard requirements table, opportunity indicators, risk flags, and one specific recommended next action. Export to PDF, share with your client.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device

Stored locally only

Your API key and business profile live in Chrome's secure local storage. They never touch BidScreen's servers — we don't have servers that see your data.

Reads one page, on click

BidScreen uses the activeTab permission: it reads only the tender page you're viewing, only when you click Screen. No browsing history, no background access.

One external call

The tender text goes directly from your browser to the Google Gemini API for analysis — exactly as if you'd pasted it into Google AI Studio yourself. Full privacy policy.
